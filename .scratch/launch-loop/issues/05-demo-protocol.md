## Question

craft, verify) vs what needs the human (vault logins via `auth
handoff`, every submit approval — submits are one-way), what
per-site record is kept (tool + `expect` markers + receipt log), and
what counts as a pass. Standing rules hold: human types passwords,
one site at a time, no new feature unless a site blocks on it.
